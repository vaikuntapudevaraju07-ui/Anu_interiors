About the Studio

Where Architectural Rigor Meets Poetic Living.

Founded on the discipline of classical symmetry and the warmth of tactile materiality, Anu Interiors creates restorative sanctuaries that quietly transcend trends.

Anu Sharma

Principal & Creative Director

EST. 2012
The Atelier Genesis

Trained at the Architectural Association in London with an early apprenticeship across Milanese ateliers, Anu Sharma established the practice in 2012. Her vision bridges austere geometric proportion with organic softness.

“A home should not demand performance; it should exhale. We treat natural light as our primary structural medium and patinated stone as our memory keeper.”

Guiding Principles

Core Architectural Pillars

01 balance

Contextual Harmony

Every spatial dialogue begins by listening to the site’s geographical orientation, microclimate, and historical architectural grammar.

02 grain

Material Honesty

Unvarnished travertine, solid hand-planed walnut, unlacquered bronze. Materials that age gracefully and tell stories across generations.

03 architecture

Tailored Craftsmanship

Custom millwork, bespoke ironmongery, and one-of-one furniture collections drafted exclusively for singular spatial footprints.

04 eco

Sustainable Longevity

Thermal efficiency through breathable lime plasters, locally reclaimed stone, and design decisions intended to endure for centuries.
